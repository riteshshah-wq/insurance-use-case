ACME INSURANCE — HOMEOWNERS POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Type: Homeowners Insurance (HO-3 Special Form)
Policy Form: ACM-HOME-2024

SECTION 1: PROPERTY COVERAGES

Coverage A — Dwelling
Covers damage to the structure of your home from covered perils.
Limit: Based on replacement cost of dwelling
Deductible: $1,000

Coverage B — Other Structures
Covers detached structures (garages, fences, sheds).
Limit: 10% of Coverage A limit

Coverage C — Personal Property
Covers your belongings inside the home.
Limit: 50% of Coverage A limit
Special sub-limits:
- Jewelry: $2,500 (unless scheduled)
- Electronics: $5,000
- Firearms: $3,000
- Cash/securities: $200

Coverage D — Loss of Use
Covers additional living expenses if your home is uninhabitable due to a covered loss.
Limit: 20% of Coverage A limit
Maximum duration: 12 months

SECTION 2: LIABILITY COVERAGES

Coverage E — Personal Liability
Covers legal liability for bodily injury or property damage to others.
Limit: $300,000 per occurrence

Coverage F — Medical Payments to Others
Covers medical expenses for persons injured on your property.
Limit: $5,000 per person

COVERED PERILS (HO-3):
Your dwelling is covered against all perils except those specifically excluded. Personal property is covered against named perils:
- Fire and lightning
- Windstorm and hail
- Explosion
- Riot or civil commotion
- Aircraft and vehicles
- Smoke
- Vandalism
- Theft
- Falling objects
- Weight of ice, snow, sleet
- Water damage from plumbing
- Electrical surge

EXCLUSIONS:
- Flood (requires separate flood policy)
- Earthquake (available as endorsement)
- Neglect and intentional loss
- War
- Nuclear hazard
- Government action
- Mold (limited coverage)
- Pest/vermin damage
- Foundation settling

CLAIMS PROCESS:
1. Call 1-800-ACME-HME within 24 hours of discovering loss
2. Protect property from further damage (board up, tarp, etc.)
3. Document all damage with photos and video
4. Create inventory of damaged/lost items with estimated values
5. Keep receipts for emergency repairs and temporary housing
6. Do not dispose of damaged items until adjuster inspection
7. An adjuster will contact you within 48 hours of filing

REPLACEMENT COST vs. ACTUAL CASH VALUE:
- Dwelling: Covered at replacement cost (no depreciation)
- Personal property: Actual cash value unless Replacement Cost endorsement is added
