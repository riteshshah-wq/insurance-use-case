ACME INSURANCE — AUTO POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Type: Personal Automobile Insurance
Policy Form: ACM-AUTO-2024

SECTION 1: LIABILITY COVERAGE

Coverage A — Bodily Injury Liability
We will pay damages for bodily injury for which any insured becomes legally responsible because of an auto accident. We will settle or defend any claim or suit for bodily injury.

Limits:
- Per Person: $100,000
- Per Accident: $300,000

Coverage B — Property Damage Liability
We will pay damages for property damage for which any insured becomes legally responsible because of an auto accident.

Limit: $100,000 per accident

SECTION 2: MEDICAL PAYMENTS
We will pay reasonable medical expenses incurred within 3 years from the date of an accident for necessary medical services.

Limit: $10,000 per person

SECTION 3: UNINSURED/UNDERINSURED MOTORIST
Covers bodily injury sustained by an insured caused by an uninsured or underinsured motor vehicle.

Limits: Same as liability limits

SECTION 4: COLLISION COVERAGE
We will pay for direct and accidental loss to your covered auto caused by collision, minus the deductible.

Deductible: $500

SECTION 5: COMPREHENSIVE COVERAGE
We will pay for direct and accidental loss to your covered auto not caused by collision, including theft, vandalism, weather events, animal strikes, and glass damage.

Deductible: $250

CLAIMS PROCESS:
1. Report the incident within 48 hours by calling 1-800-ACME-CLM
2. File a police report for any accident involving injury or significant damage
3. Document damage with photographs
4. Obtain repair estimates from approved body shops
5. Submit all documentation through the online portal or by mail

DEDUCTIBLE INFORMATION:
The deductible is the amount you pay before insurance coverage applies. Deductibles are per-incident, not per-year.

RENTAL CAR COVERAGE:
If your vehicle is undrivable due to a covered loss, rental car reimbursement is provided up to $50/day for a maximum of 30 days.

EXCLUSIONS:
- Intentional damage
- Wear and tear
- Mechanical breakdown
- Racing or speed contests
- Using vehicle for commercial purposes (unless endorsed)
- Driving under the influence of drugs or alcohol
