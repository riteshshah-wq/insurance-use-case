ACME INSURANCE — TERM LIFE POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Type: Level Term Life Insurance
Policy Form: ACM-LIFE-2024

COVERAGE OVERVIEW

This policy provides a death benefit to your designated beneficiaries if you pass away during the policy term. Premiums remain level (unchanged) for the entire term.

Available Terms: 10, 20, or 30 years
Coverage Amounts: $100,000 to $5,000,000

DEATH BENEFIT
The full face amount of the policy is paid to beneficiaries upon the insured's death during the policy term, regardless of cause (subject to exclusions and contestability provisions).

BENEFICIARY DESIGNATION
- Primary Beneficiary: Receives the death benefit first
- Contingent Beneficiary: Receives benefit if primary beneficiary predeceases insured
- Multiple beneficiaries may be named with percentage allocations
- Beneficiary changes can be made at any time by written request

PREMIUM INFORMATION
- Premiums are guaranteed level for the full policy term
- Payment frequency: Annual, semi-annual, quarterly, or monthly
- Grace period: 31 days from premium due date
- Policy lapses if premium is not paid within grace period

CONTESTABILITY PERIOD
The policy is contestable for the first 2 years from the issue date. During this period, the company may investigate and deny claims based on material misrepresentation on the application.

After the contestability period, the policy is incontestable except for:
- Fraud
- Non-payment of premiums

SUICIDE EXCLUSION
If the insured dies by suicide within the first 2 years of policy issuance, the benefit is limited to a return of premiums paid.

CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
You may convert this term policy to a permanent (whole life) policy without evidence of insurability:
- Available during the first 15 years of the term (or before age 65)
- Premiums for the new policy based on attained age at conversion
- No medical exam required

CLAIMS PROCESS
1. Notify Acme Insurance at 1-800-ACME-LIFE as soon as possible
2. Required documents:
   - Certified death certificate
   - Completed claim form (provided by Acme)
   - Original policy document (if available)
   - Beneficiary identification
3. Claims are typically processed within 30-60 days
4. Payment options: Lump sum, installments, or retained asset account

EXCLUSIONS
- Death during commission of a felony
- Death due to an act of war
- Suicide within first 2 years (limited to return of premium)

RIDERS (Available at additional cost):
- Accidental Death Benefit: Doubles the death benefit for accidental death
- Waiver of Premium: Waives premiums if insured becomes totally disabled
- Child Term Rider: Provides term coverage for dependent children
- Accelerated Death Benefit: Access up to 50% of benefit if diagnosed with terminal illness
